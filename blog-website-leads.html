Seven website improvements that help generate leads

A good website should quickly explain who you help, what problem you solve and what a visitor should do next.

1. Lead with a clear promise

Replace broad slogans with a specific explanation of the customer, service and result. A visitor should understand the business within seconds.

2. Give every service its own page

Dedicated pages give customers enough information to decide and give search engines a clearer understanding of each service.

3. Show evidence

Add relevant projects, client stories, process details and honest outcomes. Evidence builds more trust than unsupported claims.

4. Make contacting you easy

Use one clear primary action, a short enquiry form and visible phone or WhatsApp options. Ask only for information needed for the first conversation.

5. Improve mobile speed

Compress images, remove unnecessary scripts and test every important page on a real phone. Speed and readability directly affect whether visitors stay.

6. Build search intent into the content

Use the words customers use when searching, but write naturally. Titles, headings and descriptions should accurately represent the page.

7. Review the journey monthly

Measure which pages attract visitors and which actions create enquiries. Improve weak steps instead of redesigning without evidence.
