How to improve your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile can be the first thing a nearby customer sees. Keeping it complete, accurate and active helps people trust your business and take the next step.

1. Complete every important field

Use the same business name, phone number, website and address everywhere online. Select the most accurate primary category, add relevant services and write a clear description focused on what customers need.

2. Use strong, current photos

Add real photographs of your location, team, products and completed work. Refresh them regularly so customers can understand what to expect before contacting you.

3. Build a healthy review process

Ask genuine customers for reviews after successful work. Reply professionally to every review, including negative feedback. Never buy reviews or offer misleading incentives.

4. Publish useful updates

Post new services, seasonal information, offers and completed projects. Keep opening hours current, especially around holidays.

5. Measure enquiries, not only views

Track calls, website visits, direction requests and messages. Compare those actions month by month and improve the profile elements that influence real enquiries.
