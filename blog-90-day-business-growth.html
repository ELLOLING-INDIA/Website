A practical 90-day plan for small-business growth

Ninety days is enough time to identify important gaps, improve the customer journey and build better working habits. It is not a promise of instant results; it is a focused period for measurable progress.

Days 1–30: diagnose and prioritise

Review the offer, ideal customer, pricing, competitors, website, social profiles, enquiry process and daily operations. Choose a small number of high-impact priorities and define practical measurements.

Days 31–60: build visibility and lead flow

Improve core pages, Google Business Profile and social profiles. Create useful content around real customer questions, establish consistent outreach and make every enquiry easy to capture.

Days 61–90: improve conversion and operations

Follow up with leads consistently, document common objections and improve proposals. Remove repeated manual work, assign ownership and establish a weekly review rhythm.

What to measure

  • Qualified enquiries and their source
  • Response time and follow-up completion
  • Proposal-to-sale conversion
  • Website and local-profile actions
  • Operational delays and repeated tasks
